# Serveans - Roadmap & Progress

**November 2026 – March 2027**

**Last updated:** 5 November 2026

SERVE is being built and taken forward through eight streams, each contributing towards building an open, scalable and interoperable volunteering ecosystem.

This page brings together the goals, milestones and progress across these streams. It is a common space for anchors, contributors and the Working Group to understand where we are, what we are working towards and what comes next.

The roadmap will continue to evolve as we progress, experiment and learn together.

### 1. SERVE Platform

**Anchor:** Sowmya Raghuram

**Goal by March 2027:** Release Vishwas and Sampark.

#### Where we are

We started with Vriddhi, the minimum viable product, followed by Sangam, which introduced agency management, Keycloak-based authentication and support for more participants in the volunteering ecosystem.

Vishwas is the next planned release, focusing on verification, consent and credentials.

#### Milestones

| Milestone                                     | Target    | Status    |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------- | --------- |
| Vriddhi — MVP                                 | Completed | Completed |
| Sangam — Agency Management                    | Completed | Completed |
| Vishwas — Verification, Consent & Credentials | TBD       | Planning  |
| Sampark — Volunteering Fabric capabilities    | Mar 2027  | Planned   |

**What's next:** Finalise Vishwas scope, verification approach and implementation plan.

### 3. Serve Samudaya

**Anchor:** Sikha Swaroop

**Goal by March 2027:** Establish Serve Samudaya as a Community Edition pilot, bringing together:

* **10–12 Need Agencies**
* **3–5 Volunteer Agencies**
* **2–3 agencies** participating as both Need and Volunteer Agencies

#### Where we are

Serve Samudaya is being set up as a shared Community Edition of SERVE, enabling NGOs, organisations and volunteering groups to participate in the volunteering ecosystem.

Pilot infrastructure setup is underway, along with initial adopter outreach and engagement.

### 5. UP - Project SERVE

**Anchor:** Tahamul Hussain

**Goal by March 2027:** Reach 400 schools.

#### Where we are

Project SERVE is being implemented in Uttar Pradesh in partnership with the Department of Basic Education.

The initial rollout reached approximately 150 schools, connecting schools with volunteers for online teaching sessions.

The next focus is on expanding school participation, strengthening volunteer engagement and improving session delivery.

### 8. Volunteering Fabric & Seva Protocol

**Anchor:** Sowmya Raghuram

**Goal by March 2027:** Demonstrate interoperability between two volunteering platforms through a working Seva Protocol PoC.

#### Where we are

The Volunteering Fabric initiative aims to enable volunteering platforms to discover and exchange volunteering opportunities and available volunteer capacity across systems.

The initial use case has been drafted, covering both need-led and volunteer-led discovery.

The next step is to work with the NFH team on the protocol and technical implementation approach.

#### Milestones

| Milestone                                        | Target   | Status      |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------- | ----------- |
| Finalise use case with NFH                       | TBD      | In progress |
| Define technical architecture and specifications | TBD      | Planned     |
| Implement protocol integration in SERVE          | TBD      | Planned     |
| Integrate a second volunteering platform         | TBD      | Planned     |
| Demonstrate end-to-end interoperability          | Mar 2027 | Planned     |

**What's next:** Align with NFH on the technical approach and identify the participating platform for the PoC.
