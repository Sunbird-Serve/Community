# Serve - One Ecosystem, Many Streams

At the heart of SERVE is a simple belief - **everyone has something to contribute, and every contribution can make a difference.**

Rooted in eVidyaloka's journey of connecting passionate volunteers with children in rural schools, Serve is growing into a larger ecosystem where organisations, volunteers, technologists, educators and communities come together to turn their willingness to contribute into meaningful action and impact.

### Where It All Started

The journey began with **eVidyaloka**, connecting passionate volunteers with children in rural government schools to make quality education accessible.

What started as an eVidyaloka initiative gradually grew beyond the organisation, with state governments adopting the model to bring volunteer-supported learning to more students.

Through **Project SERVE in Uttar Pradesh**, volunteers support children in government schools, helping them learn and grow. The program currently covers 134 schools, with over 340 volunteering needs, 4,300 sessions delivered and 15,000+ children reached.

In **Telangana, through eVidyaNipuna**, we are extending volunteering into higher education, helping college students build digital skills and career readiness.

These programs showed that the model could work beyond eVidyaloka, with different organisations taking ownership of their own volunteering initiatives.

### Why Stop Here?

If state governments can adopt this model, **why not NGOs, community organisations and other institutions too?**

Across the country, many organisations are doing meaningful work and need volunteers to support their initiatives. What if they could also use Serve to find volunteers, coordinate activities and run their own programs, without having to build the technology themselves?

This is what led to **Serve Samudaya - The Community Edition**.

Samudaya opens Serve to more organisations through a shared, open-source volunteering platform, enabling them to run their own programs while being part of a larger volunteering ecosystem.

### What Does It Take to Make This Possible?

Bringing more organisations together requires more than just opening up a platform. We need technology that is flexible, trusted and easy to use.

That is where the **Serve Platform** comes in.

It started with **Serve Vriddhi (Growth)**, the first minimum viable product, and evolved into **Serve Sangam (Coming Together)**, bringing more participants into the ecosystem.

The next steps are **Serve Vishwas (Trust)**, strengthening verification, consent and credentials, and **Serve Sampark (Network)**, enabling a more connected volunteering ecosystem.

And as it grows, how does it help organisations coordinate more volunteers without increasing manual effort?

Through **Serve AI**, bringing agentic AI into volunteering - assisting with onboarding, matching, engagement and coordination, so people can focus more on the work that matters.

### What If Volunteering Could Go Beyond One Platform?

Imagine a volunteering opportunity published on one platform being discovered by volunteers on another.

Through the **Volunteering Fabric and Seva Protocol**, we are working towards making this possible - connecting volunteering platforms and networks so opportunities and volunteers are not limited to individual systems.

The idea is simple: **more connections, more opportunities to contribute.**

#### And What Brings It All Together?

As Serve grows across more organisations, platforms and communities, technology alone is not enough. We also need people who are equipped to contribute, opportunities to learn, and a community that keeps everyone connected and engaged.

That is where **Serve Academy and Serveans Community** come together.

**Serve Academy – Training & Capacity Building**

The training and capacity-building wing of Serve, helping volunteers, coordinators, organisations and contributors develop the knowledge and skills needed to participate effectively in the volunteering ecosystem.

**Serveans Community – Volunteer Engagement & Collaboration**

Serveans are the people who believe in the purpose of SERVE and contribute to making it happen, volunteers, educators, technologists, community builders and anyone who brings their time, skills or ideas to the ecosystem.

The Serveans Community brings these people together to connect, share ideas, learn, experiment, collaborate and contribute to different Serve initiatives.

While the Academy builds capabilities, the Serveans Community brings people together, encourages participation and keeps the spirit of volunteering alive.

Each stream has its own focus and **Community Champion**, but all are connected by one shared purpose, making volunteering accessible to more people and communities.

### The Journey Continues

From schools in Uttar Pradesh to colleges in Telangana, from enabling more organisations through Samudaya to building trusted, AI-assisted and connected volunteering infrastructure - SERVE continues to grow.

And this journey belongs to everyone who chooses to be part of it.

**One ecosystem. Many streams. Shared ownership.**

_More organisations. More volunteers. More opportunities to contribute._

**Together, we build. Together, we serve.**
