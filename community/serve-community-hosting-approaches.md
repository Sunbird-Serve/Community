# Serve community hosting approaches

We will proceed with Approach 1: retain Serveans as the SERVE ecosystem community and pilot a separate Discourse community for UP. The pilot will test programme ownership, participation and operational effort before wider rollout.

**Why Approach 1**

It fits a model where Serve enables adopter communities while maintaining a shared space for platform collaboration and learning. The choice is about ownership and community boundaries, not an assumption that one Discourse site cannot handle a large audience.

**UP pilot and review**

Keep Serveans at serveans.serve.net.in. Launch a separate UP community with a named program anchor, moderators and technical owner. Start with a manageable invited cohort and a small set of useful discussions. Keep links between the two communities; coordinators bring reusable learning back to Serveans.

Review after a proposed 4-6 weeks: activation and repeat participation, response times, moderation effort, reliability and actual operating cost. Agree the cohort, access policy, owners and review date before launch. Separate instances for further adopters remain subject to pilot learning.
