# SERVE CAPABILITIES

SERVE is built around a simple idea: **different personas perform a set of core actions to move a need from intent to delivery and impact.**

### 2. Core Verbs

| Stage                      | Core Verbs                                               | What it means                                                           |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Entry & Trust**          | **REGISTER, ATTEST, AUTHORISE, ASSIGN, ONBOARD**         | Bring people and organisations into SERVE and establish who can do what |
| **Raise Need**             | **RAISE, REVIEW, AUTHORISE, PUBLISH**                    | Create a Need, validate it and make it available for fulfilment         |
| **Discovery & Fulfilment** | **DISCOVER, REQUEST, NOMINATE, REVIEW, CONFIRM, ASSIGN** | Connect the right Volunteer to the right Need                           |
| **Delivery**               | **PLAN, DELIVER, TRACK, COMPLETE**                       | Execute and complete the contribution                                   |
| **Acknowledgement**        | **RATE, REVIEW, CREDIT, ISSUE**                          | Capture feedback and recognise contribution                             |
| **Promotion**              | **SHARE, ENGAGE, PUBLISH**                               | Make Needs, profiles, credentials and impact visible to the ecosystem   |

* **ATTEST** – confirm that an Agency, Need, Actors or claim is genuine
* **AUTHORISE** – allow a person or action to proceed
* **ASSIGN** – give a role, responsibility or Need to someone

### 3. Core Lifecycles

#### Agency Lifecycle

```
REGISTER SELF
      ↓
REGISTER AGENCY
      ↓
ATTEST AGENCY
      ↓
AUTHORISE / ADD ADMIN
      ↓
ASSIGN COORDINATORS
      ↓
ONBOARD PARTICIPANTS
      ↓
OPERATE
```

***

#### Need Lifecycle

```
RAISE NEED
    ↓
REVIEW
    ↓
AUTHORISE
    ↓
PUBLISH
    ↓
PRIVATE / SHARED / OPEN
    ↓
FULFIL
    ↓
COMPLETE
```

**Private** – visible within the Need Agency\
**Shared** – visible to selected Volunteer Agencies\
**Open** – visible to eligible Volunteer Agencies across the ecosystem

***

#### Volunteer Lifecycle

```
REGISTER
   ↓
ONBOARD
   ↓
RECOMMEND
   ↓
NOMINATE A NEED
   ↓
ACTIVE -> ASSIGNED TO A NEED
   ↓
DELIVER
   ↓
CREDIT / CREDENTIAL
```

***

#### Nomination & Fulfilment Lifecycle

```
DISCOVER NEED
      ↓
NOMINATE TO A NEED
      ↓
REVIEW NOMINATION
      ↓
CONFIRM
      ↓
ASSIGN VOLUNTEER
      ↓
PLAN
      ↓
DELIVER
      ↓
TRACK
      ↓
COMPLETE
```

***

### 4. How it all comes together

```
AGENCY
  │
  ├── establishes responsibility
  │
  ↓
NEED
  │
  ├── expresses demand
  │
  ↓
DISCOVERY & MATCHING
  │
  ├── connects supply and demand
  │
  ↓
DELIVERY
  │
  ├── converts intent into action
  │
  ↓
ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
  │
  └── records contribution and impact
```

The personas may differ, but the underlying action language remains the same.

**This combination of Personas + Core Verbs + Lifecycles forms the basic capability model of SERVE.**
